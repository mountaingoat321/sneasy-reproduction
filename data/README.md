# Independent inputs and provenance

No original model code, model input bundle, posterior samples, processed model
output, or figure data were accessed. No previous reproduction directory was
accessed. The supplied Wong PDF was read without modifying it. All numerical
results outside this directory are generated by this project's C++ model.

| Input | Origin and use | Rights and retrieval |
|---|---|---|
| `co2_annual.csv` | Annual Mauna Loa CO2, 1959–2025, transcribed from [NOAA GML](https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_annmean_mlo.txt), file dated 2026-10-09. 1959–2022 prescribes the historical boundary; 2023–2025 is held out. | Small numerical factual table, with attribution. NOAA makes these data freely available with citation. Early measurements are credited to Scripps; Mauna Loa is not a global spatial average. |
| `published_figure1.csv` | Numerical statements in Wong et al. (2025), pp. 2–3, DOI [10.1038/s44304-025-00089-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44304-025-00089-0). | Facts transcribed from prose, not digitized figures. No paper or published image redistributed. Missing upper quantiles stay missing. |
| `ocean_layers.csv` | Generated by `scripts/prepare.py` from Hooss response modes as printed in Tanaka (2007), Eqs. 2.1.11–12. | Independently computed original data. The literature supplies numerical coefficients, not a prepared model input file. |
| Historical assessment targets | IPCC AR6 WGI: 2010–2019 warming 1.06 [0.88,1.21] C; 1901–2018 sea-level rise 0.20 [0.15,0.25] m; 1971–2018 ocean heat increase 0.396 [0.329,0.463] YJ. | Facts with citation to [SPM](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_SPM.pdf) and [Chapter 9](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/chapter-9/). The first two brackets are very likely ranges; the heat bracket is a likely range. |
| Synthetic coast | Three idealized segments defined in `src/models.cpp`; no geographic locations. | Original hypothetical inputs; not DIVA, SSP4, GTSR, or observations. |

The NOAA source can be retrieved independently with `python3 scripts/retrieve_co2.py`.
The command verifies the committed numerical subset against the current upstream
file without overwriting it. Later NOAA revisions may produce a reported mismatch;
reproducing this experiment uses the committed snapshot. The offline build needs
no download. `checksums.sha256` identifies the committed input snapshot and the
supplied, excluded paper. An upstream byte checksum for browser-read sources is
unavailable; the project does not pretend that a transcription checksum is one.

## Written sources used to reconstruct equations

* Urban and Keller (2010), *Tellus A*, DOI [10.1111/j.1600-0870.2010.00471.x](https://a.tellusjournals.se/articles/10.1111/j.1600-0870.2010.00471.x): architecture of SNEASY, independent heat/carbon mixing, four terrestrial and four ocean reservoirs. The AMOC diagnostic is omitted because it does not feed this calculation.
* Kriegler (2005), [doctoral dissertation](https://www.pik-potsdam.de/members/edenh/theses/PhDKriegler.pdf), pp. 15–18, Eqs. 2.5–2.6: DOECLIM land, sea, and diffusive ocean heat equations and fixed constants.
* Ricciuto, Davis, and Keller (2008), DOI [10.1029/2006GB002908](https://doi.org/10.1029/2006GB002908), [written paper](https://pages.mtu.edu/~nurban/classes/ce5508/2009/Readings/Ricciuto08_BGC_BayesianCcycle.pdf): carbon architecture, fertilization, respiration, fixed ocean temperature variant, reference parameters. No posterior samples used.
* Tanaka (2007), [dissertation](https://e-docs.geo-leo.de/bitstreams/094e6011-fd91-44c9-855d-b31b69358ed1/download), Eqs. 2.1.11–12: Hooss ocean impulse response.
* Yi et al. (2001), DOI [10.1029/2000GL011569](https://doi.org/10.1029/2000GL011569): carbonate polynomial at salinity 35 and alkalinity 2.35 meq/kg; validity DIC 1.8–2.3 mol/m3, temperature 0–30 C.
* Fernández Villanueva and Shaffer (2025), [DCESS II](https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/18/2161/2025/), Table 3 and terrestrial model: aggregate reservoir stocks and baseline terrestrial exchange fluxes. This is an independently published replacement for unrecovered SNEASY stock constants, not proof of their equality.
* Wong et al. (2017), [BRICK](https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/10/2741/2017/), DOI 10.5194/gmd-10-2741-2017. [Written manuscript version 4](https://gmd.copernicus.org/preprints/gmd-2016-303/gmd-2016-303-manuscript-version4.pdf), appendix tables, supplied glacier parameter ranges. The preprint and final model configurations are not assumed interchangeable.
* Shaffer (2014), [DAIS](https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/7/1803/2014/gmd-7-1803-2014.pdf), DOI 10.5194/gmd-7-1803-2014, Eqs. 1–14 and Table 1: Antarctic geometry, surface mass balance and discharge. The extracted depth sign in Eqs. 10–11 is inconsistent with positive water depth. The code explicitly uses sea surface minus bed and verifies the published steady reference. This interpretation remains a source uncertainty.
* Diaz (2016), DOI [10.1007/s10584-016-1675-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10584-016-1675-4), and Wong et al. (2022), DOI [10.1029/2022EF003061](https://doi.org/10.1029/2022EF003061): coastal decision structure and accounting categories only. The synthetic cost equations are original assumptions, not implementations of their complete models.

## Missing independent inputs

The journal supplement could not be retrieved. No full DIVA exposure table,
coastal elevation inventory, SSP4 segment trajectories, fingerprint grids,
vertical land motion grid, or storm-tide time series was downloaded or used.
The independent [GTSR archive](https://data.4tu.nl/repository/uuid:aa4a6ad5-e92c-468e-841b-de07f7133786)
and [daily maxima archive](https://data.4tu.nl/repository/uuid:29614991-345e-4ffd-be22-2930912a2798)
were identified from the data creator's institutional record, but browser
retrieval failed. A single 100-year return level would also be insufficient
to determine all five return levels without additional hazard assumptions.
These data may be accessible in another environment; this is an access and
configuration gap, not a claim that they do not exist or cannot be licensed.
No unverified license is assigned to absent datasets.
